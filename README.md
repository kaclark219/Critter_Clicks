# Critter Clicks

**Chillennium 2025 Submission**  
*A cozy wildlife photography game made in Unity by team SpicySoggy.*

## Overview

In **Critter Clicks**, you play as an aspiring wildlife photographer exploring the savanna. Your mission? Capture the beauty of the wild animals around you by timing the perfect shot. While the game is designed to provide a relaxing, cozy experience, competitive players can also challenge themselves by racing against the built-in timer to see who can become the fastest photographer.

## Story

Armed with your trusty camera, you’ve set out to photograph the diverse wildlife of the savanna. Some animals will be easy to spot, while others require patience and keen observation. Complete your photo book and immortalize the savanna’s most elusive creatures!

## Gameplay Instructions

- **Open Photo Book**: Press `E`
- **Take Photo**: Left Mouse Button
- **Look Left / Right**: Press `A` and `D` or use the Left and Right Arrow Keys

## Features

- Relaxing, cozy gameplay focused on exploration and timing
- Built-in speed-run mode for competitive play
- Beautiful savanna environment with diverse wildlife
- Simple, intuitive controls
